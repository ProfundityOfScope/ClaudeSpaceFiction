Spine and slices

The ship as a city. Districts are built outward from the faces of a central spine, in tiers, at stations along its length. The ship is cut along a plane through the spine, and you look down on the cut: the two faces lying in it spread out to either side, the faces beneath show past them, and the faces above are taken away. Turning the ship brings the next pair into the cut.

Where a district is

  • Station: how far along the spine, counted from the stern.
  • Face: which side of the spine it is built on. A view has two opposite faces lying in the cut, one to each side of the spine.
  • Tier: how far out it stands. A district is built on the one beneath it.
